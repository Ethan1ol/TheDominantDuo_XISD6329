Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026. This policy covers the Legacies Screen Printers website and the Legacies Screen Printers Android app (together, "the service").

What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and contact number.
  • Google sign-in: if you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and Google's unique account identifier for you. We never see your Google password.
  • Password: if you create an email account, we store only a secure one-way hash of your password, never the password itself.
  • Orders: the designs, materials, quantities, prices and production status of the orders you place.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or payment card details, and the service shows no advertising.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To make, price and track your print orders, and to contact you about them.
  • So you see the same account and orders on the website and in the app.

We do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Where it is stored and how it is protected

Your information is stored in our database and is sent between your device and our servers over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Signing in creates a session that expires after 24 hours. On the Android app the session is kept in the app's private storage and is excluded from device backups.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and order history while your account is open, so you can see past orders. If you delete your account, we remove your personal details within 30 days. We may keep anonymised order totals for our accounting records.

Your choices

  • Update your name or contact number by contacting us.
  • Ask for a copy of the information we hold about you.
  • Delete your account and its personal data.

Children

The service is intended for businesses and adults placing print orders and is not directed at children under 13.
