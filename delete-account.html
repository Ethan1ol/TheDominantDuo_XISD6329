Delete your account

You can ask us to delete your Legacies Screen Printers account at any time. One account is used for both the website and the Android app, so deleting it removes your access to both.

How to request deletion

  1. Email privacy@legaciesprinters.example from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my account", or ask at the workshop counter (Mon-Fri, 08:00-17:00).
  2. We confirm the request is from you. We may ask you to reply from your account email.
  3. We delete your account within 30 days and email you when it's done.

What is deleted

  • Your name, email address, contact number and password hash.
  • The link to your Google account, if you used Google sign-in.
  • Your order history, once any orders still in production are completed or collected.

What we may keep

Anonymised order totals (with no name or contact details) for our financial records, as required by law.

See our privacy policy for more about how we handle your information.
