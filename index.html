Your designs, printed and tracked from order to collection.

Legacies Screen Printers prints logos, signage, decals and garments for local businesses, clubs and events. Place an order online or in our Android app, then follow it through every production stage.

Who it's for

Customers who want to order and check on print jobs without phoning the workshop, and the Legacies team who manage those orders, customers and vinyl stock.

How ordering works

  1. Sign in with Google or your email.
  2. Add your designs, choose vinyl and quantities, see the price.
  3. Track it through four production stages until it's ready to collect.

Web and app, one account

Sign in with the same Google account on this site and in the Legacies Android app: your orders and progress are the same on both.
